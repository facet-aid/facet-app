## What is FACET?

FACET (First Aid Complexity Engagement Tool) helps volunteer disaster relief organisations assess the first aid/medical capability needed in the event of a team member accident. It provides an initial evaluation of the skills and human resources required to manage potential injuries based on the specific disaster scenario.

## Using this tool, you can:

- Identify trained team members from your volunteer pool
- Enhance your risk assessment and mitigation strategy
- Make informed go/no-go decisions for the deployment
- Open discussions for partnering with other organisations or agencies who have the medical capability required.

## Please note:

- This tool is not intended to provide medical advice.
- It is based on no mitigation measures being in place.
- This demonstration app is for entertainment purposes only and is not intended for actual deployment planning.
- Use at your own risk.
