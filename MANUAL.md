## How to Use This App

The app asks seven questions about:

- The disaster location
- The disaster relief tasks to be undertaken
- The condition of the affected infrastructure
- The state of the local medical emergency services

For each question, use the pinwheel to select the most appropriate answer.

Once completed, the app will suggest the required first aid capability for the deployment. You can adjust your answers to see how different scenarios affect the first aid capability required.

## Exporting Results:

To save or share your results, press the PDF button to generate a report that can be emailed.
